## 📖 Executive Summary

**ShopSphere Analytics** is an executive commercial intelligence case study and dashboard presentation evaluating online retail growth drivers, multi-channel customer acquisition funnels, and customer lifetime value (CLV) retention metrics. Designed as a stakeholder-ready strategic deck (`ShopSphere_Dashboard_Presentation_v2.pptx`), the project synthesizes granular transaction logs into clear executive KPI cards, product category revenue contribution trees, and inventory velocity recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem & Analytical Scope

- **The Business Challenge:** E-commerce operators suffer from fragmented reporting across ad networks, cart checkout funnels, and logistics databases, making it difficult to pinpoint where revenue leaks occur in the conversion journey.
- **Analytical Objectives:**
  1. Measure Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) and Average Order Value (AOV) across seasonal sales quarters.
  2. Map the customer acquisition funnel from impression to final checkout to identify drop-off rates.
  3. Stratify customer cohorts by purchase frequency to model repurchase velocity and retention decay.
  4. Provide data-backed inventory stocking recommendations for peak shopping periods.

---

## ✨ Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Analyzed

- **💵 Financial Metrics:** Gross Merchandise Value (GMV), Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), Gross Margin %.
- **🛒 Conversion & Funnel Metrics:** Cart Abandonment Rate, Checkout Drop-off %, Click-Through-to-Purchase Conversion Rate.
- **👥 Customer Cohorts:** Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), Repeat Purchase Ratio (RPR).
- **📦 Logistics & Inventory:** Order Fulfillment SLA, Stockout Rate %, Return & Refund Rate by product vertical.

## 📊 Executive Slide Deck Structure

The presentation (`ShopSphere_Dashboard_Presentation_v2.pptx`) covers:
1. **Executive Scorecard:** Core top-line revenue KPIs and YoY expansion rates.
2. **Channel Performance Matrix:** Paid Search vs. Paid Social vs. Organic acquisition efficiency.
3. **Product Category Pareto Analysis:** Top 20% SKU lines generating 80% of net margin.
4. **Cohort Decile Breakdown:** High-value customer retention curves.
5. **Strategic Action Plan:** Q4 inventory allocation and promotional campaign calendar.
